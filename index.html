      Is the reading really free?

      Yes. Your compatibility reading is free, with no card required. When the app launches, deeper features will be available through an optional paid subscription.

      What if I don’t know our birth times?

      That’s okay. Birth times are optional. They help add detail, such as the rising sign; without them, your reading will focus on what can be interpreted from the information you provide.

      Is this just about our sun signs?

      No. Amorbit calculates both birth charts from NASA JPL planetary data and looks at how the planets in each chart relate. Interpretations are written with AI, as a starting point for reflection.

      Do we have to be in a relationship?

      You can explore a connection whether you’re dating, together, or simply curious about someone. A reading is a conversation starter, not a verdict on your future.
